01 / THE STORY

Curiosity started it.
Technology gave it a direction.

I grew up in Kabiyet, Eldama Ravine, where curiosity was always part of me. I wanted to understand how things worked — and, if possible, make them work a little better.

One of my earliest worlds was music. Around seven, I watched the keyboard teacher during breaks and practised what I had seen. That curiosity eventually opened a door into serious music learning. I learned chords, progressions, worship, seben — music became another universe.

Around Form 3, another question became impossible to ignore: what happens after school? That question pushed me toward technology. By 2023, computers had become more than machines to me — they were a way to solve problems, create, communicate and build.

From fixing a neighbor's phone to building my first website, every small win taught me something. I'm still on that journey. This website is part of it — a living record of what I'm learning and becoming.
